About Me

Infrastructure & Security Specialist with 2.5+ years running and securing hybrid environments, from AWS networks to regulated on-prem platforms. At GMV I build and operate Linux test environments on VMware vSphere and troubleshoot data transfers in cross-domain security products down to the packet level.

I'm now moving into DevOps and Cloud: completing a Master's in DevOps (UNIR) and working hands-on with Terraform, Ansible, Docker and Kubernetes. This site is self-hosted on a hardened VPS, containerised and shipped by a CI/CD pipeline I built. eJPT, ICCA and CompTIA Security+ certified.
